About the position – what will you be working on?

As a Senior AI Platform Engineer at Storebrand, you will play a key role in the development of our AI platforms and developer tools.

You'll be part of the Developer Platform team in Cloud and Security and join a strong professional community of AI experts across the group.

Here, you'll help shape how AI is used safely, effectively, and scalably—from early experimentation to production-critical services.

Here's what you'll be working on

Designing and further developing AI platforms and a "paved road" for developers
Establishing secure and compliant environments for AI development
Building a bridge between prototyping and production (Dev → Prod)
Integrating AI throughout the entire development process, not just in the code
Collaborating closely with developers, product managers, designers, and the security team
Actively contribute to professional forums and collaboration platforms across Storebrand

Who we're looking for

We're looking for someone with solid experience in platform development who understands how AI is transforming modern software development.

You've worked closely with developers, understand their needs, and are able to translate complex requirements into robust, user-friendly solutions.

You have a holistic view of the development process, are confident in technical discussions, and thrive on influencing and collaborating across disciplines and roles.
