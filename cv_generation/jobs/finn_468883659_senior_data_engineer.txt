Senior Data Engineer We’re seeking a Senior Data Engineer to join Spenn Group’s Product &amp; Tech team at our office in Oslo. With millions of users and three of the largest brands in the Nordics behind us, every point earned and spent generates data, and the insight that makes the whole ecosystem smarter runs on the platform you build. In this role, you own the data foundation turning everyday activity into insight and product value. As a Senior Data Engineer in our Data &amp; Analytics team, you will design and build the data platform that powers Spenn’s analytics, reporting, and customer experiences. You’ll create reliable pipelines and well-modeled data that turn loyalty-ecosystem data into insight and product value. You will take ownership of our data platform end to end and set the standard for data engineering across the organization. Key Responsibilities Data platform – Design, build, and operate Spenn’s data platform on Snowflake, ensuring it is reliable, performant, and scalable. Data modeling &amp; transformation – Develop and maintain robust data models and transformation pipelines using dbt, following best practices in testing, documentation, and version control. Data pipelines – Build and orchestrate ingestion pipelines that bring data from the app, platform, partners, and third-party sources into the warehouse. Data quality &amp; governance – Ensure data quality, integrity, and lineage, and support compliance with data-privacy requirements such as GDPR. Enabling analytics &amp; AI – Partner with analysts, data scientists, and product to deliver clean, well-structured datasets that power dashboards, experimentation, the recommendation engine, and customer experiences. Technical leadership – Set standards for data engineering, mentor others, and contribute to the architecture of our data platform. AI-Driven Efficiency – Utilize and advocate for AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude) to accelerate data development, documentation, and quality assurance. Collaboration – Work closely with analytics, backend, and product teams to deliver data capabilities end-to-end. Required Experience and Qualifications Bachelor&#39;s or Master&#39;s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. Several years of professional data engineering experience, at a level where you can own the data platform independently and set the standard. Strong hands-on experience with a cloud data warehouse, ideally Snowflake (deep experience with Databricks, BigQuery or Redshift is also highly relevant, as the fundamentals matter most). Strong proficiency with SQL and dbt (or a similar transformation framework such as SQLMesh or Dataform). Experience building and orchestrating ELT/ETL pipelines. Solid understanding of data modeling and data warehousing best practices. Programming skills (e.g., Python) for data tooling and automation. Familiarity with cloud infrastructure (AWS) and BI tools is a plus. Experience in fintech, loyalty, or other data-privacy-sensitive domains is an advantage. Active, demonstrated use of AI tools (e.g. Claude, ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot) to work more efficiently. This is a genuine expectation at Spenn, not a nice-to-have. Full professional proficiency in Norwegian (Danish or Swedish also fine) and English. Day-to-day work in Norwegian, while all documentation is in English. Experience in fintech or other regulated environments is an advantage. Personal Attributes Strong sense of ownership, with a focus on data quality, reliability, and scalability.
