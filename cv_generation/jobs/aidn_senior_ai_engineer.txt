Senior AI Engineer - Aidn
Location: Oslo, Bergen, Bodø, Stavanger, or anywhere in Norway (Hybrid)
Source: https://aidn.teamtailor.com/jobs/7232832-senior-ai-engineer

Why this role matters
Aidn is building an integrated healthcare platform for municipalities, healthcare professionals, social workers, and citizens to collaborate. The role contributes to a "digital colleague" that helps with information retrieval, administrative automation, and better decision support.

Role focus
- Build shared AI capabilities in production for healthcare workflows.
- Deliver reliable, auditable automation with safety guardrails.
- Ship measurable time savings for healthcare professionals.

What you will work on
- Workflow orchestration: version-aware state machines, observability, audit trails, reliability.
- Automation engine and guardrails: safe execution, previews, human-in-the-loop checkpoints, risk tagging, rollback, traceability.
- Applied AI in workflows: speech-to-text, summarization, drafting, contextual QA linked to correct patient/case/data context.
- Agentic and multi-step automation: controlled goal-driven chained task flows with strict safety boundaries.
- Shared platform building blocks: reusable components, APIs, evaluation patterns, tooling.
- Evaluation and monitoring: test and monitor AI and automation quality, reliability, trust over time.

Current tech stack
- Languages/frameworks: .NET/C# backend, Python for AI and evaluation tooling.
- Cloud/AI platform: Microsoft Azure, Azure AI services, Azure AI Foundry.
- Workflow/automation: shared state-machine workflow engine and automation platform.
- Data/storage: PostgreSQL, Azure Storage, structured JSON payloads.
- Messaging/streaming: Kafka.
- Infra/CI/CD: GitHub Actions, ArgoCD, Terraform, Atlantis.
- Observability: Loki, Grafana, Tempo, Mimir (LGTM stack).

Must-haves
- Strong software engineering experience in production systems.
- Hands-on LLM and/or speech-to-text product experience.
- Experience moving AI from prototype to production (reliability, latency, cost, failures).
- Evaluation mindset and regression prevention.
- Cloud experience (preferably Azure) and observability familiarity.
- Strong cross-functional communication.

Nice-to-haves
- Agentic systems or multi-step AI workflows.
- Production AI monitoring (quality, cost, latency, drift, failure modes).
- Azure AI Foundry familiarity.
- Internal platform work used by multiple teams.
- Regulated environment experience with auditability/risk controls.
- Workflow orchestration/state machines/event-driven systems familiarity.

Success criteria
- 3 months: understand workflow and governance requirements, own and improve a platform area, build trust across teams.
- 6 months: deliver production/late-stage AI platform capabilities, ship at least one guarded AI workflow step, improve evaluation and monitoring.
- 12 months: contribute to a production "digital colleague" workflow with measurable time saved; scale shared capabilities across teams.

Company context
- Healthcare domain with safety, traceability, and trust as non-negotiable.
- High ownership, flexibility, and platform growth stage.
- Salary range listed: 1.150-1.350 MNOK/year.
