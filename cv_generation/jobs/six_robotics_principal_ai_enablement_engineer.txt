Principal AI Enablement Engineer - Six Robotics
Location: Oslo
Source: https://careers.sixrobotics.com/jobs/7724691-principal-ai-enablement-engineer

About Six Robotics
At Six Robotics, we build intelligent software that gives Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) the ability to collaborate, adapt on the fly, and execute missions that were previously impossible. From Oslo, a team of 70+ experts in defence, software, and AI develops autonomy and swarm platforms used by government and industry.

About the role
The mission is to establish a sovereign AI setup. This may involve building from scratch, adapting open-source solutions, or working with a certified provider that can replace cloud-based frontier models for core organizational needs. The role sits in a designated AI Enablement function and works closely with the CTO, Head of IT, and internal DevEx and Tooling team.

What you will do
- Identify and drive bold initiatives for how to leverage AI across the organization.
- Drive technical implementation aligned with defense-context compliance and sovereignty requirements, including securing, operating, and documenting the solution.
- Build and operate an internal AI stack that other teams can build tools and services on top of.
- Lead hands-on implementation across the stack.
- Conduct internal training and help teams use AI effectively in daily work.
- Stay current on AI developments and bring relevant ideas in-house.

What they are looking for
- 8+ years of software engineering experience, including at least 4 years relevant to AI enablement, MLOps, or applied AI.
- Track record leading a meaningful AI transformation or building an AI enablement function from scratch.
- Strong, current knowledge of AI tooling landscape and LLM ecosystem.
- Strong engineering rigor: coding, system design, correctness.
- Experience designing scalable AI platforms balancing growth, security, maintainability, access control, and auditability.
- Strong communication with technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Ability and interest to stay current and evaluate AI developments.
- Fluent English.
- Master's degree or equivalent practical experience.

Nice to have
- Experience with sovereign AI setups or on-prem LLM infrastructure.
- Experience with self-hosted compute stacks and GPU infrastructure.
- Prior experience running internal AI training or workshops.

Why join
- Work on meaningful UAV autonomy problems with real-world impact.
- Drive AI and scalable software modernization in hardware-dominated environments.
- Build from zero and shape systems, tools, and culture.
- Competitive compensation and mission-driven team.

Recruitment process
1. Screening interview (30 min)
2. Team meet & greet (60 min)
3. Technical home assignment, tests, and technical interview (90 min)
4. Final interview with CTO and Head of People (45 min)

Security clearance required for all employees due to collaboration with Norwegian Armed Forces.
