The Vision Technology team develops sensor solutions using monocular and stereo camera technologies deployed on GPU-enabled Linux platforms. These sensor solutions are integrated into larger automation and robotics systems that improve operational efficiency, enhance safety, and enable advanced autonomous functionality.
