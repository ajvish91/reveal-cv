# ForwardMedia / University of Boston postdoc context

## Centre

**ForwardMedia: Research Centre for Responsible Media Technology & Innovation** (University of Boston, host; Boston's Media City)

- **Mission:** Develop responsible media technology, especially AI for the media sector.
- **Motivation:** Address ethical issues in online media (echo chambers, political polarisation, contested election outcomes) while leveraging AI where it adds value.
- **Research areas:** Media user engagement, content production, content interaction and accessibility, audience understanding (methods and metrics).
- **Outputs:** Patents, prototypes, papers, software; research training in media technology and innovation; long-term impact including start-ups.
- **Consortium:** Major US media industry partners; University of Boston is the host institution.

## Democracy base project (candidate work)

**Democracy base** is a project within ForwardMedia. LLM pipelines summarize administrative documents for **media personnel** (classification, content understanding, summarization, evaluation). Emphasis on locally hosted fine-tuned models, data control, privacy, and cost versus external APIs.
